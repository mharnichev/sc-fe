# Accepted-card indicators

These are static acceptance marks, not payment-method buttons. Artwork bytes are unchanged; CSS preserves aspect ratios and original colors. The checkout displays the blue Visa mark and full-color Mastercard Symbol on a light surface, with proportional sizing and comparable prominence. The white Visa asset is retained for dark surfaces. No filters, recoloring, cropping or stretching.

- Visa white wordmark: https://www.visa.com/vcom-assets/content/dam/visa/header/Vector.png — public official Visa site asset, discovered in https://www.visa.com/en-us on 2026-10-22.
- Visa standards: https://corporate.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/corporate/about-visa/documents/visa-brand-standards-sept2025.pdf (white on dark backgrounds; proportional sizing and clear space).
- Mastercard full-color Symbol (41×26 SVG): https://www.mastercard.com/content/dam/brandcenter/assets/images/logos/mclogo-for-footer.svg — public official Brand Center asset, obtained from its rendered page asset inventory on 2026-10-22.
- Mastercard requirements: https://www.mastercard.com/brandcenter/us/en/brand-requirements/mastercard.html (full-color Symbol for merchant acceptance, unaltered artwork, clear space, equal prominence).

Trademarks remain the property of their owners. No unrestricted asset license is asserted; use is limited to accepted-card identification in this merchant checkout. The gated downloadable artwork agreement/forms were not submitted or accepted.

- Visa blue checkout wordmark (`visa-blue.png`): official Visa asset retained from https://www.visa.com/vcom-assets/content/dam/visa/header/vectorblue.png — retrieved 2026-11-01 and used unaltered on the light checkout surface.
- Monobank checkout wordmark (`monobank.svg`): official monobank wordmark retained from https://monobank.ua/ header inline SVG — retrieved 2026-11-01, original paths/viewBox preserved and currentColor resolved to black for the light checkout surface.
