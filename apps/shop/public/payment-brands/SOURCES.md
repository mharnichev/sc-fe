# Accepted-card indicators

These are static acceptance marks, not payment-method buttons. Artwork bytes are unchanged; CSS preserves aspect ratios and original colors. Visa white is displayed on a dark surface; both marks have at least 18px clear space and comparable prominence. No filters, recoloring, cropping or stretching.

- Visa white wordmark: https://www.visa.com/vcom-assets/content/dam/visa/header/Vector.png — public official Visa site asset, discovered in https://www.visa.com/en-us on 2026-10-22.
- Visa standards: https://corporate.visa.com/content/dam/VCOM/corporate/about-visa/documents/visa-brand-standards-sept2025.pdf (white on dark backgrounds; proportional sizing and clear space).
- Mastercard full-color Symbol (41×26 SVG): https://www.mastercard.com/content/dam/brandcenter/assets/images/logos/mclogo-for-footer.svg — public official Brand Center asset, obtained from its rendered page asset inventory on 2026-10-22.
- Mastercard requirements: https://www.mastercard.com/brandcenter/us/en/brand-requirements/mastercard.html (full-color Symbol for merchant acceptance, unaltered artwork, clear space, equal prominence).
